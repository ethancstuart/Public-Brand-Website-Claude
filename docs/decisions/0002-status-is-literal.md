# 0002 — Status is literal, and unflattering statuses stay

**Date:** 2026-08-22 · **Status:** accepted, with a known cost

## Context
Portfolio copy drifted toward implying momentum that did not exist ("eight
products in flight"). Several products have no users.

## Decision
Four literal statuses: `live` (a stranger can use it today), `invite` (real users,
gated), `building` (not in anyone's hands), `paused`. Never inflate one.

## Why
A register whose statuses are aspirational is worth less than no register. The
honesty is the credibility.

## Consequences
- Products say "not yet in anyone's hands" on a page selling shipping ability.
- **Known cost, surfaced 2026-08-23 by a simulated VP reviewer:** the honesty
  reads well, but four of seven products in nobody's hands *"doesn't neutralize
  that — it certifies it."* The mechanism is sound; the **inventory** is the
  problem. Fixing it means shipping or cutting, not rewording.
