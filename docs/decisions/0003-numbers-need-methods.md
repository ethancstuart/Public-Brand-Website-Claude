# 0003 — A figure carries its method, or it is cut

**Date:** 2026-08-22, amended 2026-08-23 · **Status:** accepted, partially unmet

## Context
The site had carried unverifiable figures. The blanket rule "no exact numbers"
was then applied so broadly it deleted *checkable* ones too (NexusWatch's
"45+ live data layers across 86 countries" became "global country coverage").

## Decision
Two categories of figure are allowed:
1. **Sourced** — carries its measurement method inline (Operating Record).
2. **Independently checkable** — a reader can confirm it in under a minute from a
   link already on the page.

Everything else is cut, not hedged. Product counts never appear in copy.

## Why
The rule exists to stop unverifiable boasting, not to make the site vague.
Specificity that survives checking is the whole point.

## Rejected
Softening checkable figures "to be safe" — reversed on 2026-08-23. Vagueness
costs credibility without buying safety.

## Consequences — including a live gap
Declaring this standard on the page **invites the audit**, so every gap between
the standard and the substance reads as chosen rather than accidental.

⚠ **Currently unmet:** the Operating Record tile `4 → 1 / Months from spec to
shipped` carries the source *"before and after the AI-native operating model"* —
a restatement, not a method. No denominator, baseline definition, or window.
Either give it a real method or soften the declaration. Open.
